How to draw... a pineapple

Start with one bold fruit shape, attach five pointed crown leaves, then give it dark sunglasses and a sideways grin. Two tiny rind marks and bright felt-tip fills finish this playful 15-minute doodle.

15 minEasy
Start doodling

Marker ready?

Let's draw a pineapple

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • White drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Golden-yellow, bright-green, and dark teal-blue markersUse the listed fill colors

Outline the body, crown, face and rind marks with black felt tip. Let the ink dry, then add one broad color pass so small white paper streaks remain visible.

  1. 01

    Shape the pineapple

    Draw one upright rounded pineapple body, wider in its lower half, and flatten its base slightly. Close a narrow oval rim across its top where the crown will attach.

    Marker tip: The narrow top rim gives all five future crown leaves a firm place to start.

  2. 02

    Grow five crown leaves

    Behind the oval top rim, grow five connected pointed leaves; make the middle leaf tallest and fan two shorter leaves to either side. Keep their bases touching the rim.

    Marker tip: Count five tips: one tall middle leaf and two on each side.

  3. 03

    Put on the sunglasses

    High on the body, draw two broad rounded sunglasses lenses joined by a short bridge. Beneath them, add one off-center crooked smirk instead of dot eyes.

    Marker tip: Keep the face playful with a slanted smirk rather than adding dot eyes beneath the shades.

  4. 04

    Mark the rind and ground

    Put one small V-shaped rind mark on each lower side of the body, clear of the smile. Add a few broken shadow strokes below the flat base.

    Marker tip: Place one V on each side, then count three separated ground strokes.

  5. 05

    Fill the tropical colors

    Fill the established fruit golden yellow, its five crown leaves bright green, and the sunglass lenses dark teal-blue. Keep the crooked smirk, two V-shaped rind marks and three ground strokes visible through the marker color.

    Marker tip: Color around the black outlines and preserve a few pale streaks in the yellow fill.
