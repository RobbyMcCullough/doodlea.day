How to draw... a firefly in a jar

Draw one clear jar, then build a floating firefly from a tiny head, dark thorax, long glowing tail, and two veined wings. Four glow dashes and bright marker color make the light readable without complicating this 20-minute doodle.

Let's draw a firefly in a jar

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • White drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Gray markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Yellow, orange, coral, teal, and pale-blue markersUse the listed fill colors

Use black felt-tip for the jar and firefly contours. Let the ink dry, then use pale color on the wings and glass and build the tail glow with yellow and orange marker strokes.

  1. 01

    Shape the glass jar

    Draw a wide oval mouth and one plain narrow rim band. Drop two jar sides from its shoulders, round the lower corners, and join them with a flat bottom. Place two short gray shine strokes inside each side.

    Marker tip: Keep the rim wider than the jar body center, and leave enough white paper inside for the bug.

  2. 02

    Mark rim and base

    Draw short vertical rib marks across the existing rim band. Echo the flat bottom with one shallow inner curve just above it, keeping the jar and four side accents in place.

    Marker tip: Space the lid ticks loosely; the inner base curve sits above the outer bottom.

  3. 03

    Place the firefly

    Place a small blank round head near the jar upper middle. Attach a short gray thorax beneath it, then a long unstriped abdomen tapering to a point; leave the head empty for the next step.

    Marker tip: Keep the head blank now so its expression appears at the same time as its wings.

  4. 04

    Give it wings and a face

    From each thorax shoulder, curve an oval wing outward and add light gray vein strokes inside each. Attach two antennae to the head, then draw a left dot eye, right wink, and small smile.

    Marker tip: Root both wings at the gray thorax and aim their long tips toward the jar sides.

  5. 05

    Show the tail glow

    Place two short yellow glow dashes on each side of the tail tip and tint the established tip pale yellow. Beside the existing gray jar accents, add one pale blue gleam on each side.

    Marker tip: Count two dashes on each side of the tail; keep the yellow mostly inside the existing contour.

  6. 06

    Color the glowing firefly

    Fill the established rim coral, head teal, wings pale blue, and tapered abdomen yellow to orange toward its tip. Deepen the same four glow dashes to orange; keep the jar mostly white and the four gray accents and two pale blue gleams visible.

    Marker tip: Use visible marker strokes in the tail instead of a perfectly smooth digital gradient.
