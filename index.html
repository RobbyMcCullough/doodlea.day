How to draw... a giraffe

Build the long head-and-neck silhouette in one marker pass, attach ears and ossicones, then give this giraffe a sideways look and crooked two-tooth grin. Four spaced neck patches and a short mane make the portrait recognizable before golden marker color. This easy 20-minute doodle practices connected contours, repeated spots and directional fills.

Let's draw a giraffe

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Golden-yellow, warm-brown and coral-pink markersUse the listed fill colors

Draw every contour with black felt tip and let it dry. Use short directional golden marker strokes inside the neck, brown inside the four spots and mane, and coral inside the ears; keep the two teeth white.

  1. 01

    Shape the head and neck

    With a black felt tip, draw a long upright neck that widens into a rounded head. Push one blunt muzzle to the left and join it under the chin; leave four small open gaps at the top for two ears and two ossicones.

    Marker tip: Pull the two neck edges in one relaxed pass. Count four tiny breaks across the top before lifting the marker.

  2. 02

    Attach ears and ossicones

    At the four top gaps, attach two leaf-shaped ears angled outward and two short stalks tipped with round ossicones between them. Keep the muzzle and neck in place.

    Marker tip: Join each ear and stalk to an open top endpoint; count two ears and two round-ended stalks.

  3. 03

    Give it a side glance

    Inside the same head, draw one large round eye with a heavy half-closed upper lid and pupil looking sideways right. Add two solid black nostrils on the muzzle and an open crooked grin showing two square teeth.

    Marker tip: Set the eye first, then keep the two nostril dots separate from the toothy smile.

  4. 04

    Place the neck spots

    Draw exactly four irregular dark-edged spots down the long neck, stepping them from the jaw to the shoulder and leaving clear gaps between them.

    Marker tip: Count four separate patches from top to bottom; leave yellow space between every outline.

  5. 05

    Add mane and cheek marks

    Along the back of the neck, add a short row of little triangular mane tufts. Put three cheek freckles below the eye; keep both black nostril dots unchanged.

    Marker tip: Keep the freckles small and well clear of the upper patch; let the mane lean out from the back edge.

  6. 06

    Color the giraffe

    Fill the existing head and neck golden yellow, the four spots and mane warm brown, and the inner ears soft pink. Keep the same black outlines, two nostril dots, teeth and freckles.

    Marker tip: Pull color strokes along the long neck and stop before they cover the small cheek freckles.
