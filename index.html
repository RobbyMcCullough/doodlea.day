How to draw... a black cat

Arch one playful cat body, attach a small head and four grounded paws, then curl the tail and give the face a toothy grin. Black felt-tip texture and amber eyes finish this lively 20-minute drawing.

20 minEasy

Marker ready?

Let's draw a black cat

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Amber markerUse the listed fill colors

Use the black marker for the arched contours, ears, four legs, tail, face and uneven body fill. Add amber to the two eyes while leaving the grin and left whiskers as paper highlights.

  1. 01

    Arch the back

    Start near the left rump, sweep a high arch toward the right shoulder, and add a shallow belly curve below it. Leave the ends open for the head, legs and tail.

    Marker tip: Keep the belly curve shallower than the back arch so the pose reads clearly.

  2. 02

    Attach the head

    At the right shoulder, round a small cheek and muzzle facing right. Raise two pointed ears from the head top and join the neck to the existing back.

    Marker tip: Leave the left rump open for a tail and the lower edge open for four legs.

  3. 03

    Set down four paws

    Drop two short front legs under the head and two back legs under the rump; end each with a rounded paw and keep all four on one ground level.

    Marker tip: Count two rear paws and two front paws before moving on.

  4. 04

    Curl the tail

    From the left rump, lift a thick tail upward and curve its tip left; return the inner edge to the same rump attachment without crossing the back.

    Marker tip: Keep the tail tip inside the page and clear of the cat ears.

  5. 05

    Give the cat a grin

    Place two slanted almond eyes, a tiny triangle nose and a broad grin with two small teeth. Flick three whiskers from each cheek without moving the head.

    Marker tip: Use slanted eyes and a toothy grin, not dot eyes and a U-smile.

  6. 06

    Fill the cat with marker

    Fill the established cat silhouette with uneven black marker strokes, leave slim paper highlights on the back and tail, and tint the existing eyes amber. Keep the grin and whiskers clear.

    Marker tip: Let marker streaks show, and preserve both sets of whiskers as light or dark contrast.
