How to draw... a cartoon cupcake

Start with the wrapper and frosting as two connected shapes, leaving a small opening for the cherry. Add the pleats and sprinkles, then color the established forms. This 15-minute marker lesson practices clean overlaps and bright felt-tip texture.

Let's draw a cartoon cupcake

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • White drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Raspberry pink, bright red, and teal markersUse the listed fill colors

Let the black outlines dry before filling the frosting pink, cherry red, and wrapper teal. Leave small white streaks of paper in each marker fill.

  1. 01

    Shape the wrapper and frosting

    Draw a low tapered wrapper with a shallow curved rim. From both rim ends, curl uneven frosting scallops upward, stopping short of the top center to leave a round gap for the cherry.

    Marker tip: Leave a round opening between the two frosting sides for the cherry.

  2. 02

    Top it with a cherry

    Fill the reserved top-center gap with a round cherry, joining its lower sides to the two frosting curves. Add one short bent stem above it; keep every earlier line.

    Marker tip: Let the cherry bridge the open frosting gap without drawing through it.

  3. 03

    Fold the wrapper

    Inside the wrapper, pull four short pleat lines from the curved rim toward the flat bottom. Space them unevenly and keep them inside the outline.

    Marker tip: Count four separate pleats across the wrapper.

  4. 04

    Sprinkle and ground

    Scatter five short separate sprinkle strokes over the frosting, avoiding the cherry. Beneath the wrapper, draw a short broken ground shadow.

    Marker tip: Count five sprinkles before drawing the three ground marks.

  5. 05

    Color the cupcake

    Fill the established frosting with streaky raspberry pink, the cherry red, and the wrapper teal. Keep five black sprinkles and four black pleats visible inside the color.

    Marker tip: Color inside the contours with broad strokes that leave a little white paper.
