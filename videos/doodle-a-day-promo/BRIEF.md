## Intent

Create a short promotional video for X that uses the real Doodle a Day tutorial
experience as its proof. Open with Robby's story about wanting to draw a cowboy
hat for Tracie's handmade birthday card, then move quickly into a “you can draw
this” progression that makes the daily tutorials feel approachable and worth
trying. Keep the personal origin to roughly the first five seconds so most of
the 30-second runtime demonstrates the drawing steps.

The tone should feel conversational, personal, playful, and lightly produced —
not like a conventional ad or generic AI-product launch.

## Assets

- https://doodlea.day/ — capture the live site's real design, tutorial screens, and artwork.
- User-recorded voice-over — record after the storyboard and final script are approved.
- Original birthday-card or cowboy-hat photo — optional if the user supplies one later; do not block production on it.

## Customizations

- Feature captured site screens and real tutorial-step artwork as the main visual evidence.
- Use styled captions so the video works when watched muted in the X feed.
- Use the user-recorded voice-over and subtle bundled marker-and-paper sound effects; do not use HeyGen. Build the cut without a music dependency.
- Defer the design-system choice until after site capture, then derive it from the site's actual colors, typography, paper texture, and marker art.
- Use live storyboard and rough-layout review before animation, followed by final pre-render approval.

## Notes

- Minor timing, copy, and production changes are delegated to the agent when they improve the result.
- Do not use or ask about HeyGen for this project.
- Keep the tutorials, rather than the AI implementation, as the star of the video.
- Avoid generic SaaS-demo aesthetics and avoid turning the piece into an argument about AI art.
