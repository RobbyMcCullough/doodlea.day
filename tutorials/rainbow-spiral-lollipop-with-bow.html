How to draw... a spiral lollipop

Start with a big round candy and wind one clean spiral inside it. Tie a two-loop bow beneath the candy, pull the stick behind its knot, then add two sparkles and streaky rainbow marker color in this playful 20-minute lesson.

Felt-tip marker mode

Let's draw a spiral lollipop

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Thick black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Pink, yellow, teal, orange, purple, and gray markersUse the listed fill colors

Draw the candy, swirl, bow, stick, and sparkles in black felt tip. Let the ink dry, then fill the established areas with short marker strokes that leave a little paper grain visible.

  1. 01

    Draw the round candy

    Near the top half of the page, draw one generous hand-drawn circle. Leave open paper below it for the bow and stick.

    Marker tip: Keep the circle comfortably high so the bow and stick have room below.

  2. 02

    Wind the swirl

    Start a loose black spiral near the candy center and wind it outward toward the rim. Stop before crossing the outer circle.

    Marker tip: Turn the page as you pull the spiral in one relaxed motion; stop at the rim.

  3. 03

    Tie the bow

    Directly under the candy, draw a small oval knot, a rounded loop to each side, and one short pointed ribbon tail beneath each loop. Keep all four parts attached to the knot.

    Marker tip: Count two loops and two pointed tails, all touching the same oval knot.

  4. 04

    Pull the stick

    From below the bow knot, draw two straight parallel stick edges down and round their lower tip. Stop the top edges behind the bow so no line crosses either loop.

    Marker tip: Begin beneath the knot and stop both stick sides at the same rounded end.

  5. 05

    Add two sparkles

    Outside the round candy, draw one small four-point sparkle on the left and one on the right. Place three short broken shadow strokes just below the stick tip.

    Marker tip: Keep both sparkles outside the candy and three shadow marks below the stick.

  6. 06

    Color the candy and bow

    Let the black outlines dry. Fill the existing spiral spaces with bright pink, yellow, teal and orange marker, color the bow purple, the stick warm gray and both sparkles yellow. Keep the same swirl and bow contours visible.

    Marker tip: Work around the black swirl line and let small white paper streaks show.
