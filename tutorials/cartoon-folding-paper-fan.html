How to draw... a folding paper fan

Shape one bold fan, divide it into sections, add scalloped trim and a flutter, then color the established wedges with textured markers.

15 minEasy
Start doodling

Felt-tip marker mode

Let’s draw a folding paper fan

Treat this as one playful practice round: sketch the idea loosely, simplify the shapes, then commit with confident marker outlines and bright fills.

Grab your stuff

Materials

  • Drawing paperSmooth paper helps marker lines
  • Black felt-tip markerUse the listed fill colors
  • Coral, yellow and teal markersUse the listed fill colors

Ink the stable outer fan and ribs first; let black outlines dry before filling the wedges with visible marker strokes.

  1. 01

    Shape the fan

    Draw a broad curved fan leaf with two sides meeting a round pivot and a short handle below.

    Marker tip: Set the pivot below the leaf so the handle has room.

  2. 02

    Divide the leaf

    From the pivot, draw three straight inner ribs to the curved upper rim.

    Marker tip: Space three inner ribs around the center one.

  3. 03

    Add the trim

    Draw a scalloped decorative arc just inside the existing upper rim.

    Marker tip: Keep the outer rim untouched.

  4. 04

    Place two dots

    Add one small black dot in each lower outside wedge.

    Marker tip: Keep both dots inside the lower wedges.

  5. 05

    Show a flutter

    Draw two short motion ticks outside the fan’s upper-left edge.

    Marker tip: Leave a paper gap beside the ticks.

  6. 06

    Color the fan

    Fill the existing fan sections with alternating coral, yellow and teal marker strokes, preserving the black ribs, dots and motion ticks.

    Marker tip: Let marker texture show; preserve the black lines.
